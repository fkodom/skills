# Content and product proof

## Write the brief first

Use `assets/content-brief.md`. Specify one reader question, the decision or task it serves, relevant existing pages, sources, the original contribution and the evidence still needed. Choose the format after inspecting intent. A comparison needs useful comparison criteria; a setup guide needs actual steps; a troubleshooting guide needs tests and next actions.

Keep broad discovery content distinct from promotional blog work when the project uses both. A learning/resource collection can sit outside the main blog feed while remaining linked, readable and crawlable. “Hidden” must not mean presenting different facts to crawlers, hiding keyword blocks or publishing orphan pages merely to inflate volume.

## Choose the useful format

| Reader wants to… | Deliver on the page | Check before creating it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Use a template | Blank template, completed example and adaptation instructions | Can it be used without visiting a list of providers? |
| Perform a task | Working input/output or a clear manual procedure | Does the next step preserve the task and entered information? |
| Choose a service | Scope, setup, evidence and meaningful tradeoffs | Is the offer relevant to this reader? |
| Find examples | Inspectable examples with useful categories | Are the examples real, distinct and permitted to share? |
| Learn a process | A direct explanation followed by actual steps | Does a beginner have enough context to start? |

This decision aid synthesizes [Blitz](sources/blitz-seo-1.md), [Good Enough Logos](sources/goodenoughlogos.md) and [Trendin](sources/trendin.md). A search phrase is an input to research, not an instruction to produce a blog post. Keep the page useful before its sales invitation.

## Lead with the answer

Use plain nouns and verbs. Explain an unfamiliar term once. Give the reader the answer before the background. Default to short, descriptive subheadings; follow the project's editorial conventions. Remove paragraphs that restate the heading or delay the practical step.

Add length only when it helps the task. No fixed word count or title-character target establishes quality. Avoid boilerplate conclusions, keyword-stuffed headings and ungrounded urgency. [People-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) and [title guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link).

## Make the answer inspectable

Select a useful asset:

- A completed worksheet alongside the blank version.
- A labeled fictional call and the resulting summary or record.
- A diagram showing who receives a call and where its information goes.
- A short product recording tied to a specific claim, with readable supporting text.
- A comparison table with meaningful distinctions and plain qualifiers.
- A decision tree or checklist that produces a concrete next step.

Use existing product UI when available. Mark mockups, fictional data and untested scenarios. Avoid exposing client records or turning decorative AI imagery into supposed product proof. Inspect final assets on mobile; do not count an image buried in metadata as a visible example.

Match proof to the output: an audio product needs an audible example; a generated-video product needs a video; a workflow needs its inputs and resulting record. Record comparable inputs, settings and observed failures. A narrated test can supply an article, screenshots and a short demonstration without inventing separate evidence for each format. See [Verbatik](sources/verbatik.md) and [AIApply](sources/aiapply.md).

For directory or data pages, ask which fields let a visitor make a decision, how facts are verified, and when they are updated. A large record count or many filter combinations cannot replace useful, distinct pages. Never turn private customer records into public search assets without permission. See [Fundbat](sources/fundbat.md) and [Hormozi](sources/hormozi.md).

For location-based answers, useful differences may be access instructions, facilities or current restrictions, each with a source and update owner. For metric-based pages, explain the measure, inputs, freshness and limits. Compare fields against the reader's task before adding more prose. [Fast Pass Driving Tests](sources/fastpassdrivingtests.md) and [Coindive](sources/coindive.md).

A utility must deliver the promised input and useful result; do not substitute promotional copy for the task. Its separate page needs a distinct workflow, not just another keyword. [PopaDex](sources/popadex.md) and [Botster](sources/botster.md). Improve the task's outcome before expanding the template.

## Build from real knowledge

Capture an operator explaining or demonstrating the task. Extract the decisions, examples and failure cases; verify them; then use AI to organize and simplify the article. Retain the relevant source recording or notes privately. This supplies first-party substance without asking a model to invent experience. See [AI Suit Up](sources/aisuitup.md).

For an approved public dataset, sketch its relationships before generating pages: an entity, a useful category, a reverse lookup or a dated aggregate. Give each proposed view a reader question, required fields, source, update owner and expiry rule. Start with a few representative pages and check whether they answer different questions. [Hoverify](sources/hoverify.md) supplies an architectural example, not a reason to expose customer data or generate every combination.

Google's current guidance focuses on accuracy, relevance and added value, rather than treating all AI-assisted content as a violation. Human review must cover the claims and metadata as well as the prose. [Generative AI guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content).

## Compare without guessing

Choose criteria that matter to the buyer, not rows invented to award the sponsor more checkmarks. Separate documented capability, directly tested behavior, configuration-dependent support and unknown information. “Not documented in the sources reviewed” does not mean “does not exist.” Cite current official product sources near claims and record access dates.

State our verified strengths directly. Preserve specific useful competitor descriptions. Remove a throwaway row when neither side supplies a meaningful answer. Claims about quality, setup speed, price, certifications or integrations require an appropriate basis. An unnamed competitor in a customer anecdote cannot support a named comparison. Exact quotes and public-use permission must be verified before publication.

## Match the next step

A reader researching a problem may benefit from a worksheet or related procedure before a sales call. Keep the essential answer usable on the page. Put a natural next step beside the task it helps, without an intrusive overlay that hides the answer. [Interstitial guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/avoid-intrusive-interstitials).

Before drafting another page, inspect whether the existing page lacks a usable example, current fact, internal link or suitable offer. Ship the smallest meaningful improvement and record why it is expected to help. Content volume alone is not evidence of business progress.
