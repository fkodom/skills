# Diagnose before expanding

## Establish the baseline

Write down the actual business objective: qualified inquiries, booked demonstrations, useful trials or another verified outcome. Identify the reader, decision-maker, market, existing distribution, product boundaries and conversion definition. Exclude known tests. Ask what happened before the buyer knew the category existed.

Inventory live pages, drafts and open changes. Record the exact production URL, reader job, format, owner and status. Confirm deployed content rather than treating a merged pull request as a verified release. Retain previous URL moves and canonical decisions so an expected redirect is not misclassified as a new problem.

## Locate the weak step

| Step | Evidence to collect | Useful next question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Sitemaps, internal links, external references | Can a reader and crawler find this exact URL? |
| Fetching | Direct GET, robots rules, rendered browser view | Is the page accessible, complete and intended for publication? |
| Indexing | Dated Search Console inspection and chosen canonical | Does Google report the intended page as indexed? |
| Relevant retrieval | Query/page data and neutral search samples | Does the page answer the job expressed by this query? |
| Trust | Independent relevant references and visible proof | What can a buyer verify without taking our word for it? |
| Action | Landing-page behavior and genuine conversions | Is the next step useful for this reader's stage? |

These can overlap. Do not declare backlinks the sole cause because the site is new, or conclude that a registrar transfer fixes ranking without an observed DNS/hosting failure. Separate brand confusion, old-domain migration and nonbranded demand.

## Prioritize evidence over scores

For each candidate action, record:

- What is observed, on which URL, on what date?
- What reader task or business result does it affect?
- What alternative explanation remains?
- What is the smallest useful change, its owner and effort?
- What would make us retain, revise or stop it?

Fix a verified block to important live content before expanding the content queue. Otherwise prefer actions that help the reader even if rankings do not move: a clearer product example, a correct third-party description, a usable worksheet, or a more relevant next step.

Use impact, confidence, effort and dependencies as separate judgments. An unexplained weighted SEO score hides uncertainty. A ranking fluctuation alone is insufficient to reorder the entire strategy.

## A compact strategy output

Deliver a baseline table, three or fewer evidenced gaps, an ordered work queue, and the next action. Distinguish implementation from proposals. Name unavailable evidence and the retrieval route needed; do not repeatedly request broad access when a narrow export would suffice.

Reassess the limiting step after a meaningful change or enough comparable observation time. Avoid fixed promises such as “indexed in 48 hours” or “more pages means more inbound.”
