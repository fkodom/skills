# Reader intent and query panels

## Start before the category

Interview sales/support staff, inspect consented customer questions, and sample public discussions and search results. Extract the user's own problem language. A law-firm operator may begin with “why do prospective clients not call back?” or “how to get a new firm found locally,” long before searching for an answering service. These are research examples, not claims of measured demand.

Map a journey with concrete jobs:

| Stage | Job | Candidate format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before an inquiry | Be found, trusted and easy to contact | Setup aid, diagnosis, worked checklist |
| Problem recognition | Understand missed calls or slow follow-up | Troubleshooting guide, decision tree |
| Solution exploration | Choose staffing, forwarding or automation | Options comparison, call-flow illustration |
| Product evaluation | Check fit, quality, setup and handoff | Demonstration, sourced comparison, test plan |
| Operation | Configure and improve a real workflow | Procedure, example record, debugging guide |

Keep a clear connection to the audience. Large traffic for an unrelated utility is not necessarily useful acquisition. Avoid rewriting every topic as a product pitch.

## Build a panel, not a pile

Use `assets/query-panel.csv` as a starting schema. Assign stable query IDs, exact phrases, intent cluster, stage, market, language, device/context and a version. Record why each belongs: observed customer wording, first-party impressions, recurring source appearances or a hypothesis requiring validation.

Keep a small benchmark set constant and rotate broader clusters on a defined schedule. Fifty or more questions can be a research inventory without fifty daily samples. Allocate coverage by business importance and available sampling time. Include problem questions, alternatives, comparisons, operational tasks and brand queries; report each separately.

Treat synonyms as a cluster until observed results or reader needs justify different pages. Do not assign one new URL per keyword. Keep a suggested destination for editorial planning, but count the company's actual appearing URL in measurement.

Check ambiguous terms against the actual results and audience. A physical service and a similarly named software service may share a phrase while answering different needs. Likewise, an expatriate and a foreign applicant may need different instructions even when their searches overlap. An estimated zero-volume phrase remains a research candidate when a concrete customer question supports it; it is not proof of demand. See [Cloud Residents](sources/cloud-residents.md).

## Inspect the results themselves

For a small representative set, read actual high-visibility primary pages. Record the page's reader, opening answer, structure, example, proof, currentness, author/source basis and next step. Separately note source types: vendor, publisher, directory, community, official documentation or video.

Ask what a reader still cannot do after reading them. A gap is a hypothesis within this inspected sample, not a market-wide vacancy. Useful contributions may be a completed example, honest tradeoff table, tested workflow, original dataset or a practical tool. Rewording the competitor's prose is not a contribution.

Match distribution to that task too: a video demonstration, specialist discussion or creator review may serve it better than another general article. Inspect the audience and commercial relationship before proposing participation. [Paytience](sources/paytience.md) supplies examples, not evidence of guaranteed reach or permission to post.

Competitor paid keywords are another source of hypotheses. They show observed bidding, not profitable conversion or suitable intent. Confirm the audience and actual page before adding a term. For example, joining an affiliate program and purchasing affiliate-management software are different jobs. See [Tolt](sources/tolt.md).

## Validate demand before scaling

A plausible phrase can lead to the wrong audience. Record the original customer wording, observed result types, category versus brand intent, country/language and any demand estimate with its source. AI suggestions are candidates, not measurements. Missing volume is unknown; repeated mismatched results are a reason to reconsider the term or distribution channel. [Niche Tools](sources/nichetools.md) and [StealthGPT](sources/stealthgpt.md).

When expanding internationally, compare the market you can actually serve. Keep global volume separate from the target country's results and demand. [Italian Gourmet Shop](sources/italiangourmetshop.md). Do not infer reachable customers from worldwide searches.

## Decide build versus update

Match the job to the inventory before creating a brief. Update the strongest existing page if the intent overlaps. Create a new page for a distinct need that merits its own answer. Consolidation requires checking actual traffic, links, canonicals and redirects; do not delete an older page solely because its title resembles another.

Maintain 5–10 actionable ideas rather than a large unqualified writing queue. Each needs evidence, contribution, required product/customer proof, next step, owner and status. Reconcile completion states against real PR and deployment evidence.
