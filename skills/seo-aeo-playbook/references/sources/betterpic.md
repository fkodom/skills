## Application and limits

For each priority asset, identify the claim or evidence a publisher would reference. Keep affiliate relationships distinct from editorial evidence. Local pages must accurately represent location and services. Reject promised rank changes from one backlink and demographic-image ranking speculation. URL Rating is a proprietary page metric, not simply an internal-link count; verify its definition with the tool provider.
