# BetterWorld

Source: [SEO Roast](https://seoroast.com/betterworld). Recorded June 20, 2024; read October 15, 2026. All 349 available segments through 14:58. No new screenshot or video-frame review.

## Useful observations

- [4:00](https://seoroast.com/betterworld?t=240): Customer-hosted fundraising pages create a different link source from editorial citations to the company's blog. Analyze these separately.
- [6:00](https://seoroast.com/betterworld?t=360): A provider can be named in a ranking article without receiving a link; verify both facts.
- [8:00](https://seoroast.com/betterworld?t=480): Overlap among relevant competitors is a useful topic shortlist. Group variants by the actual destination page and intent.
- [14:00](https://seoroast.com/betterworld?t=840): A publisher's donation-provider link reflects an operational relationship. It is not automatically an available editorial placement.

## Application and limits

Separate product-driven distribution, publisher citations and brand mentions in the link register. Check why a link exists before pitching. Do not adopt coordinated ranking-link exchanges or infer causation from domain ratings. Historical competitor traffic estimates cannot establish present demand, indexing or achievable Lighthouse outcomes.
