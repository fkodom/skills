- **2:00–18:00:** Inspect the actual destinations behind a search result. A vendor can be discovered through its own page, a marketplace or a publisher's comparison. Those are different visibility outcomes.
- **16:00–18:00:** Separate people joining an affiliate program from businesses buying software to run one. Similar words can describe opposite sides of a market.
- **19:00–25:00:** A tool-seeking query needs a clear product task and output. Vague time-saving language does not establish what the product does.
- **26:00–38:47:** A template query needs a usable template. A list of template providers can leave the immediate job unfinished.

Application: choose the destination and useful asset before drafting. Show the product category, a real example and the next step. Marketplace eligibility, badges and named expert review must be verified. A 24-hour ranking check or repeated indexing request cannot establish that one edit caused a gain.
