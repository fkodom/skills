# Cal.com: make comparisons useful

Source: [SEO Roast audit](https://seoroast.com/cal). Recorded 2025-11-28; reviewed 2026-10-14 through 22:13. Transcript and chapter text reviewed.

- **6:28–11:20:** slow openings and thin comparisons delay the reader's decision.
- **11:26–12:20:** durable URLs reduce avoidable future changes.
- **15:17–16:41:** buyer-specific needs can justify distinct pages.

Application: use concise criteria, useful examples and relevant product evidence. Keep established URLs unless a move has a concrete benefit and migration plan. A competitor's login traffic is not necessarily acquisition demand. Traffic-value estimates are not revenue, and self-first rankings or link-pumping microsites are not a substitute for substantiated product fit.
