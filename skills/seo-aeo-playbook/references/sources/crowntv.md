# CrownTV

Source: [SEO Roast](https://seoroast.com/crowntv). Recorded July 28, 2024; read October 15, 2026. All 136 available segments through 14:17. No new screenshot or video-frame review.

## Useful observations

- [4:00](https://seoroast.com/crowntv?t=240): A large existing article inventory can still have weak visibility. Review the page and its actual competing answers before adding more topics.
- [6:00](https://seoroast.com/crowntv?t=360): Cluster overlapping competitor queries into reader tasks; a raw keyword export contains redundant variants.
- [9:00](https://seoroast.com/crowntv?t=540): Separate homepage link analysis from links to individual guides. Generic expert mentions may be less relevant than a citation in a closely related article.
- [10:00](https://seoroast.com/crowntv?t=600): Trace the source's topic, linked destination and reason for citing that particular asset.
- [13:00](https://seoroast.com/crowntv?t=780): A simple visual showing the product in use can explain more than additional homepage prose.

## Application and limits

Record contextual fit and citation purpose, not only domain scores or counts. Keep natural brand anchors. The recording does not prove the links caused ranking differences; paid ranking links and indiscriminate comment placement are not recommendations adopted by this playbook.
