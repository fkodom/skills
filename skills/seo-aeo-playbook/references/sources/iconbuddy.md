## Application and limits

Use descriptive visuals and contextual navigation where the content warrants them. Treat asset credits as a distinct link mechanism. Image metadata can support attribution and eligible appearances; Open Graph tags or schema do not guarantee image rank. Reject automatic traffic multipliers and boilerplate expansion unsupported by useful, accurate asset information.
