## Application and limits

Use localized, page-specific comparison and useful supporting details. Keep worldwide volume separate from reachable demand. A competitor recommendation is evidence to study, not permission to insert promotion. Reject fixed backlink targets and promises that a few links or a metadata change will secure a particular rank. Structured data creates eligibility for supported appearances, not guaranteed ranking uplift.
