# Niche Tools

Source: [SEO Roast](https://seoroast.com/nichetools). Recorded June 19, 2024; read October 15, 2026. All 333 available segments through 16:27. No new screenshot or video-frame review.

## Useful observations

- [2:00](https://seoroast.com/nichetools?t=120): A demonstration tool should return useful guidance, not just a bare score or copied page metadata.
- [6:00](https://seoroast.com/nichetools?t=360): Plausible category language repeatedly returns the wrong buyer intent.
- [8:00](https://seoroast.com/nichetools?t=480): AI-generated keyword suggestions do not establish audience demand or the intended results.
- [12:00](https://seoroast.com/nichetools?t=720): A full reader question finally surfaces more relevant examples, despite uncertain measured volume.
- [15:00](https://seoroast.com/nichetools?t=900): A product without clear category demand may need audience education and existing distribution channels rather than endless keyword variants.

## Application and limits

Record mismatched intent and missing demand evidence. Missing tool volume is unknown demand, not proof that nobody searches. Use real customer phrasing and observed results to qualify ideas; stop forcing a terminology-led SEO strategy when repeated evidence does not support it. Channel changes remain proposals requiring their own authorization.
