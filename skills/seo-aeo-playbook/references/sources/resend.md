# Resend: keep audience relevance

Source: [SEO Roast audit](https://seoroast.com/resend). Recorded 2024-08-18; reviewed 2026-10-14 through 18:57. Public transcript and chapter text reviewed.

- **1:02–1:48:** a descriptive category helps explain the product.
- **11:23–13:29:** useful audience questions deserve space alongside announcements.
- **14:21–14:52:** large unrelated traffic may not produce buyers.
- **15:30–16:08:** a separate resource collection can organize educational pages.

Application: keep the learning collection useful and discoverable even when it is outside the promoted blog feed. Choose broad topics only when their audience relationship is clear. This example supports testing reader fit, not assuming every top-of-funnel visit will convert.
