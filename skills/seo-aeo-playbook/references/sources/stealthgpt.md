# StealthGPT

Source: [SEO Roast](https://seoroast.com/stealthgpt). Recorded June 25, 2024; read October 15, 2026. All 393 available segments through 14:14. No new screenshot or video-frame review.

## Useful observations

- [3:00](https://seoroast.com/stealthgpt?t=180): Sending an unfamiliar visitor directly to sign-in can omit the explanation or result their query promises. Match the landing experience to intent.
- [6:00](https://seoroast.com/stealthgpt?t=360): Inspect an actual referring article and the product's placement before deciding whether inclusion is plausible.
- [10:00](https://seoroast.com/stealthgpt?t=600): A phrase resembling a category may actually carry competitor-brand intent. Check results before assigning it to the homepage.
- [13:00](https://seoroast.com/stealthgpt?t=780): For an interactive-tool query, the expected input and result may matter more than a promotional video.

## Application and limits

The transferable lesson is intent and experience matching, not the product's detection claims. Reject paid ranking links, bought list positions and guaranteed ranking implications. A referring/outgoing-domain ratio cannot establish that a publisher sells links. Exact-match domain effects, AI penalties and conversion estimates are presenter hypotheses, not demonstrated facts.
